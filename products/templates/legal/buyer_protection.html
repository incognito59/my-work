Buyer Protection Policy

Your peace of mind matters. RedCart is committed to making online shopping safer, clearer, and more trustworthy for buyers across Nigeria.

Quick summary: we support secure payments, clear product information, delivery tracking, dispute review, and refund or return assistance when an order is incorrect, damaged, or not as described.

1. Secure Payments

All payments are processed through trusted payment providers and protected by secure checkout flows. We never ask buyers to share full card details outside our verified payment channels. Payments are only completed through the official checkout process on RedCart.

2. Product Quality Assurance

We encourage sellers and product listings to provide accurate descriptions, clear photos, and honest pricing. If an item you receive does not match the listing, is defective, or is materially different from what was advertised, you may contact support for assistance.

3. Delivery and Order Tracking

Orders are tracked where available, and customers receive status updates throughout the fulfillment process. If an order is delayed, missing, or marked delivered without arrival, our support team will review the issue and help resolve it.

4. Returns and Refunds

Customers may request a return or refund within the applicable policy window for items that are damaged, incorrect, or materially different from the listing. Refunds are processed after verification and confirmation by our support team.

5. Dispute Support

If you believe your order has an issue, contact our support team immediately with your order number, product details, and evidence such as photos or screenshots. We will review the concern and help facilitate a fair resolution.

6. Your Rights as a Buyer

  • Receive accurate product, pricing, and delivery information
  • Use a secure checkout process and trusted payment methods
  • Request support for incorrect, damaged, delayed, or missing orders
  • Ask for a review of disputes and a fair resolution path

7. Contact Us

If you need help with a purchase, please reach out to our support team at afolabiprosper329@gmail.com or through the contact page. We aim to respond promptly and assist with order concerns, disputes, or refund requests.

RedCart is building a safer shopping experience for Nigerian buyers, one order at a time.
