Buyer Protection Policy

Your peace of mind matters. RedCart is committed to making online shopping safer, clearer, and more trustworthy for buyers across Nigeria.

1. Secure Payments

All payments are processed through trusted payment providers and are protected by secure checkout flows. We never ask you to share your full card details outside our verified payment channels.

2. Product Quality Assurance

We encourage sellers and product listings to provide accurate descriptions, clear photos, and honest pricing. If the item you receive does not match the listing, you can contact support for help.

3. Delivery and Order Tracking

Orders are tracked where available, and customers receive status updates throughout the fulfillment process. If an order is delayed or missing, our support team will assist you.

4. Returns and Refunds

Customers may request a return or refund within the policy window for items that are damaged, incorrect, or not as described. Refunds are processed after verification and confirmation.

5. Dispute Support

If you believe your order has an issue, contact our support team immediately with your order number, product details, and evidence such as photos or screenshots. We will review and help resolve the matter.

6. Your Rights as a Buyer

  • Receive accurate product and pricing information
  • Be informed about payment, shipping, and delivery expectations
  • Request support for incorrect, damaged, or delayed orders
  • Use our customer support channels for dispute resolution

7. Contact Us

If you need help with a purchase, please reach out to our support team at afolabiprosper329@gmail.com or through the contact page.

RedCart is building a safer shopping experience for Nigerian buyers, one order at a time.
