Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better shopping experience by remembering your preferences and actions.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for cart and checkout functionality
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your theme preference (light/dark mode)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Session Cookies: Keep you logged in during your visit
  • Security Cookies: Protect against cross-site request forgery (CSRF)

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
sessionid Maintains your login session Session (2 weeks if "Remember Me")
csrftoken Protects against cross-site request forgery 1 year
theme Remembers your light/dark theme preference 1 year
cart Stores your cart items Session
recentlyViewed Saves products you've viewed 30 days

Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Paystack: For secure payment processing
  • Google Analytics: For website analytics (if enabled)
  • Bootstrap CDN: For website styling

We do not control third-party cookies. Please check their respective privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete individual or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Set browser to block all cookies

⚠️ Important: Disabling cookies may affect:

  • Cart functionality (items may not be saved)
  • Login persistence (you'll need to log in each time)
  • Theme preferences (dark/light mode won't be remembered)
  • Recently viewed products (history won't be saved)

How to Manage Cookies in Different Browsers

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data
  • Edge: Settings → Site permissions → Cookies and site data

Cookie Duration

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain for up to 30 days or until manually deleted
  • Long-term Cookies: Some preferences (theme) stored for 1 year
